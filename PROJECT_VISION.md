# 🎤 THE VOCALYST VISION

# Vision

Vocalyst is an AI-powered speech rehabilitation platform designed to support individuals experiencing speech and communication challenges. Its primary goal is to make speech therapy more accessible, affordable, and engaging through intelligent technology.

The platform aims to assist people recovering from medical conditions that affect speech, including:

- Dysarthria
- Apraxia of Speech
- Aphasia
- Articulation Disorders
- Stuttering (future support)

Beyond rehabilitation, Vocalyst is also intended to help anyone who wants to improve their speech clarity, pronunciation, fluency, and communication confidence through AI-powered analysis, personalized exercises, and progress tracking.

**Important Note:** Vocalyst is designed to assist speech practice and rehabilitation. It is not intended to replace professional diagnosis or treatment provided by certified speech-language pathologists.
